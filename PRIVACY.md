# 🔒 Privacy Policy

**Status:** Active Development

OpenCrosshair is designed with a **privacy-first** approach.

### 1. Zero Tracking
- **No Accounts:** You will never need to register or log in.
- **No Telemetry:** OpenCrosshair collects no usage stats, IP tracking, or analytics.

### 2. Community Hub
- When presets are shared, only the visual configuration data (colors, size, shapes) is stored. No personal identifiers are linked to submissions.

### 3. Contact & Questions
For any privacy questions or issues, please open an issue directly in our GitHub repository under the **Issues** tab.
